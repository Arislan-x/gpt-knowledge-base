退款政策

生效日期：2026 年 7 月 25 日

付款处理方

Paddle 是 GPT 知识库付费交易的 Merchant of Record（登记商户），负责收款、税费、收据、取消和退款。退款申请由 Paddle 按照其 Refund Policy、Buyer Terms 和适用法律处理。

申请退款

用户可以通过 Paddle 交易确认邮件中的“查看收据”或“管理订阅”链接申请退款，也可以访问 paddle.net 并选择退款支持。请提供购买邮箱、交易编号和申请原因。不要在 GitHub Issues 中公开付款凭据或完整交易资料。

适用条件

  • 退款资格以购买地的强制性消费者保护法律和 Paddle 在交易时有效的政策为准。
  • 在适用情况下，中国大陆消费者可依 Paddle 政策和法律在交易后 7 日内主张相应取消权；其他地区可能适用不同期限和条件。
  • 如重大技术缺陷导致已购买的云服务持续无法使用，请先提交技术支持请求。问题无法合理解决时，可向 Paddle 申请退款。
  • 超过法定或政策期限、已经充分使用的数字服务、滥用、欺诈或违反服务条款的请求，可能不符合退款条件。

取消与退款的区别

取消月度自动续费套餐会阻止下一周期扣款，通常不会自动退还当前周期费用。一次性“12 个月”套餐不会自动续费。退款获批后，相关付费权益可能立即终止或按 Paddle 的处理结果调整。

退款到账

获批退款通常退回原付款方式，实际到账时间取决于 Paddle、付款方式和金融机构。开发者不会要求用户通过私人转账收取或退回 Paddle 订单款项。

联系

付款、收据和退款：Paddle Buyer Support。产品技术问题：GitHub Issues。

Refund Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

Payment provider

Paddle is the Merchant of Record for paid GPT Knowledge Base transactions. Paddle handles payment, tax, receipts, cancellation, and refunds. Requests are processed under Paddle's Refund Policy, Buyer Terms, and applicable law.

How to request a refund

Use the View receipt or Manage subscription link in the Paddle transaction email, or visit paddle.net and choose refund support. Provide the purchase email, transaction number, and reason. Do not post payment credentials or full transaction details in GitHub Issues.

Eligibility

  • Eligibility is governed by mandatory consumer law in the place of purchase and Paddle's policy in effect for the transaction.
  • Where applicable, consumers in mainland China may exercise the relevant cancellation right within seven days under Paddle's policy and applicable law. Other locations may have different periods and conditions.
  • If a material technical defect persistently prevents access to purchased cloud service, contact product support first. If it cannot reasonably be resolved, a refund may be requested from Paddle.
  • Requests outside statutory or policy periods, substantially consumed digital services, abuse, fraud, or violations of the Terms may not qualify.

Cancellation is not a refund

Canceling a monthly auto-renewing plan prevents the next charge and does not normally refund the current period automatically. A one-time “12 months” plan does not renew. Approved refunds may end or adjust paid access immediately according to Paddle's decision.

Refund timing

Approved refunds are normally returned to the original payment method. Timing depends on Paddle, the payment method, and the financial institution. The developer will never ask buyers to receive or return Paddle order funds through a private transfer.

Contact

Payment, receipt, and refund support: Paddle Buyer Support. Product technical support: GitHub Issues.
